Privacy Policy

BasitBiOyun Publisher · Last updated: 26 October 2026

BasitBiOyun Publisher ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the BasitBiOyun football channel to send the channel's own edited videos to the owner's TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, used only to confirm the right account is connected;
  • an access token and refresh token, used only to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts through the video.upload scope.

How the data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file outside any shared folder. Nothing is sent to any server operated by us, and no database or analytics service is used.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
